PROJECT 01 SMART INSOLE — RUNNING
01 / 04
June 2026–PresentTeam Lead | Design Engineer

Smart Insole — Running

Concept: The smart insole is designed as an injury-prevention device. Placeholder sensors measure activity data, which is analysed alongside characteristics such as age, weight, gender, and foot gait. The insole provides feedback that can inform a training regime.

Explore concept

Illustrative brief: Explore how a running insole might provide useful impact feedback while staying comfortable during high-impact sport.

Design questions: Where could sensing elements be positioned, how might the insole fit different users, and how could feedback be useful?

Areas to investigate: Sensor placement, comfort, durability, data interpretation, and prototype testing with users.
